CollectiveStrike3D

Twenty division operators, ten arenas, one spike. Choose an operator, buy a loadout, and take six rounds off a bot squad that reads how you play. It runs entirely in this browser tab, offline, with nothing to install.

FULL BRIEFING BELOW
01

How a round plays

Tactical mode is a spike match. One squad carries the spike to a site and defends the timer, the other squad hunts them down or defuses it. Four beats repeat until one side has six rounds.

Staying alive

02

Three playlists

The same movement, aim, and ability layer drives all three. What changes is who you are fighting and how many of them arrive at once. Pick one here and it carries into selection.

03

The operator roster

Each division is a Collective AI company translated into a body, a role, an active ability on E, a passive that is always running, and its own doctrine weapon. Filter by role, then click one to take it straight into selection.

04

The arsenal

Four buyable base weapons cover the economy, every operator brings an original signature and a newly forged Ascendant divisional weapon, and Series 03 adds a shared pool. Slot 6 cycles the expanded armory; every damage figure below is live runtime data.

Doctrine weapons

Series 03 + Ascendant armory

05

Ten arenas

Every arena keeps the same learnable competitive layout — two sites, two spawns, readable rotations — and rebuilds everything else: architecture, hazards, lighting, weather, and the way shots travel over elevation.

06

Apex bosses

Apex bosses use locomotion classes no operator has — colossus, swarm host, phase, orbital, storm, mirror, helix titan. Each telegraphs its signature before it commits, so the counterplay is positioning rather than reflex.

07

Controls and intensity

Keyboard and mouse, touch twin-stick, and standard dual-stick gamepads are all live. Combat intensity changes bot aim, reaction time, and how often they spend abilities — set it here or from the pause menu.

08

Ready when you are

Career level, operator mastery, arena records, and best streak save to this browser. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.
