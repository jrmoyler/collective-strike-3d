# Kenney Environment Assets

The curated GLBs in this directory come from the official Kenney downloads
listed below. All three packs are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0
1.0), permitting personal, educational, and commercial use.

| Local folder | Official pack | Version | Runtime use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `industrial/` | [City Kit (Industrial)](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial) | 1.0 | Forge stacks/tanks and the Forge/Neon perimeter skyline |
| `space/` | [Modular Space Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/modular-space-kit) | 1.0 | Grid-snapped Forge/Neon wall shells and gate arches |
| `cave/` | [Modular Cave Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/modular-cave-kit) | 1.0 | Grid-snapped Cryo/Verdant wall shells, rock gates, and organic perimeter pieces |

Only the low-poly modular pieces used by the runtime are committed. The original
pack archives, FBX files, OBJ files, previews, and unused room models are not
included.
